The calculations were not changed. That was verified in three ways.

1. **Printed diagnostics.** Every script was run before and after the revision,
   in float64 and, for experiments 4, 7, 8, 9 and 11, also at `--precision mp50`,
   together with `smoke_test.py`. All 87 diagnostic lines printed by the archive
   versions reappear verbatim and in order in the revised output. The one
   deliberate exception is Experiment 7's progress message, which now cites
   eq. (76) where the archive cited eq. (67).
2. **Diagnostic table.** Experiment 11's `moving_family_diagnostics.csv` is
   byte-identical before and after, in both float64 and mp50.
3. **Notebook regression.** `scripts/test_notebooks.py` compares each script's
   named results with its marimo playground to `rtol = 1e-11` and checks plot
   counts. It passes against the revised scripts.

The revised scripts also print new explanatory diagnostics. Experiment 1 now
reports its interpolation error away from the killed boundary, and Experiment 5
fits a decay rate. Experiment 6 adds a refinement ladder that repeats its check
at ½×, 2× and 4× resolution. These are additions beside the original
calculations, not changes to them.

`source-hashes.json` records the current SHA-256 of every Python file in this
directory, so `scripts/check_release.py` still detects unreviewed edits. Its
entries are now the reviewed hashes, not the archive hashes; the archive hashes
are listed above.

## Figures

`figures/` holds the SVGs generated by `run_all.py` from the revised scripts.
They are deterministic, so a rerun on the pinned environment reproduces them
byte for byte. No plots or CSV files from the original archive are included.

## Environment

The uv environment uses the repository-root `pyproject.toml` and `uv.lock`, with
marimo in the optional `notebooks` dependency group. The requirements files in
this directory remain compatibility snapshots.
