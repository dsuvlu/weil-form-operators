What is computed where

Scenes 1–3 are computed live in your browser from the paper’s closed forms: the prefix sums behind eq. (44), the exact antiderivative of the kernel (7), and the Riemann sum (42). Scenes 4–6 replay values computed beforehand in high-precision arithmetic (python-flint/Arb and mpmath). Scene 4 uses the exact multiplier (53); its whole-line energy is symmetric under σ ↦ 1 − σ to nine digits. Scene 5 is the matrix of Theorem 9 at x = 10, N = 10. Scene 6 uses ground states of the literal matrices whose simplicity and evenness were checked in ball arithmetic.
